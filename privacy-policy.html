What the game stores

The game does not require a player account. Progress, settings, inventory, currency and daily rewards are stored in app storage. On Android, a second copy of gameplay progress is kept in private native app preferences to recover from WebView cache or storage loss. Purchase-delivery records remain in private app storage. Android cloud backup is disabled. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes this local information and can permanently remove consumed virtual items.

Age and privacy settings

The game is intended for teens and adults, not marketed to children under 13. The optional date-of-birth screen calculates an age band on the device. Only the band is saved; the date is discarded. Under-13 and unknown-age users receive no native ads or purchases. Teens receive non-personalized ad requests with under-age-of-consent treatment. Paid random rewards and return-to-app ads are restricted to adults in this release. These settings are product safeguards, not a claim that all countries define childhood identically.

Advertising and third-party data

Where enabled, Google AdMob and its consent service process data for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. This may include IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, ad interactions and diagnostic information. Non-personalized advertising does not mean no data processing. Native ad requests are blocked unless the consent service permits them.

You can access Privacy & Terms → Ad privacy choices in the game where applicable, and your device's advertising/privacy settings. Google determines its own retention periods under its policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's partner-services information.

Purchases

When enabled, Google Play handles checkout, payment details, pricing and purchase records. The developer does not receive your card details. The app processes the product ID, signed purchase receipt and purchase token to verify and deliver an item, then records a receipt hash to prevent duplicate delivery. Undelivered tokens are retained locally until the purchase is consumed. Purchase availability and region eligibility may use the country returned by Google Play; it is not GPS location. Google retains its transaction records under its policies.

Permissions and connections

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or shared-storage access. Internet/network access supports ads and purchases; vibration supports controls. Gameplay assets and model decoders are bundled locally. Choosing external community links opens another service whose own privacy terms apply.

Support, retention and rights

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and anything you include. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary personal data. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve requests, prevent abuse and satisfy applicable legal obligations; deletion requests can be sent to the contact above. Local gameplay data remains until you clear it. There is no developer-hosted player-account database or player-account deletion process.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal information, object to processing, withdraw consent, obtain portability, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact the developer for information held through support; contact Google for information Google controls. Clearing app data or resetting an advertising ID does not necessarily delete Google's records or replace these rights. Services may process information outside your country under their applicable safeguards.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in this policy and, where required, presented in the app. For child-privacy concerns or other privacy questions, contact Muhammad Kashif at the address above.
